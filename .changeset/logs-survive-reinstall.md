`ovd logs` and Debug → Logs restart the log stream after every install OVD makes (turn it off with `--no-restart-after-install` or the footer toggle), reconnect by themselves when the device drops, and show OVD's own events (installs, launches, crashes, restarts, reconnects) as OVD lines.
