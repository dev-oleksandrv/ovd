Inspect .vpkg files: `ovd pkg info [file]` shows a package's ID, title, version, build, architecture (`any` for pure-JS packages), build type and SHA-384, and `ovd pkg list` lists the open project's builds. The desktop app gets the Packages screen with a drop zone, recent builds and the Overview tab.
