- Remote & Screen shows the target's screen. The Virtual Device plays live ("Live · emulator gRPC") and follows your remote presses as they happen; the view pauses while the window is hidden.
- On a Fire TV stick it shows the latest screenshot with its time and a Refresh button, marked "Out of date" after a key press. The stick screenshot isn't checked on a stick yet.
- "No signal" shows while the device is offline.
